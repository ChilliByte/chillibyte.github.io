On the
problem

Twenty-one dots arranged 3 across by 7 down. Of the C(21,2) = 210 straight lines between pairs, seventy are degenerate — they pass through another dot along the way — leaving 140 distinct, indivisible segments to work with.

The abstract possibility space is 2¹⁴⁰ ≈ 1.4 × 10⁴² on/off combinations — an unimaginable expansion of the 2×4 grid's four million. What concerns us is still the small subset that resolves into readable English: 26 capitals, 26 lowercase, 10 digits, and the workaday punctuation.

This specimen ships unencoded. The wider grid relieves the 2×4's worst collisions (B/D, V/X, I/l) and opens up middle-column strokes, but the design choices are now yours to make. Click any cell to open the editor and start setting segments.
